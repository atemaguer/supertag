# Code of conduct

SuperTag follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests, and discussions, and anywhere you
represent the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@atemaguer](https://github.com/atemaguer). Reports
are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, issues, or
contributions, and may restrict or ban participants who break this code, following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.

Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not here.
